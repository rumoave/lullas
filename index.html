A letter from our founders

Queridos amigos,

On June 24, two earthquakes hit northern Venezuela less than a minute apart. The strongest our country has felt in a century. Homes fell in La Guaira, in Caracas, along the coast. Families are still sleeping outside.

We started collecting in our own dining rooms and kitchens here in Brooklyn. Then our neighbors showed up. Then their neighbors.

Recovery takes years, not weeks. This is a long road, and we intend to walk all of it.

Walk it with us.

Con amor,

The families of Casa Ora & Lulla's Bakery
